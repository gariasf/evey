# evey

A kitchen terminal and its backoffice.

- **The terminal** is a dedicated screen on the fridge door. It shows recipes, runs cooking sessions step by step with timers, and knows what is in the pantry and the fridge ("can cook now").
- **The backoffice** is where recipes, the pantry, leftovers and weekend plans are edited.

**Status:** charting. The first map, *Foundations and ladder*, will decide four things: the terminal's concept, its hardware, the architecture, and the milestone order.
