The conductor within equipment limits

The question. Given recipes shaped as ADR 0006 says (steps with equipment, timers marked walk-away or hands-on, hands-on minutes spread over untimed steps), can a conductor run a session inside the kitchen's equipment, get the hot dishes to finish together, and keep the cook on one thing at a time? Press Next as the cook would. Every recipe here is invented; the kitchen is invented too.

Rules on trial: a vessel named by consecutive steps is held from the first to the last; a pot, pan, small pot or fryer also takes a burner; food goes on the heat only once its prep is ready; a hands-on step is never interrupted; when food on the hob will buzz soon, the cook gets a task that ends before it; dishes eaten now are held so their heat ends with the rest; a soak or marinade of 2 h or more happens the day before.
